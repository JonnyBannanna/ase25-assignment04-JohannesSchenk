# How does the prototype attempt to address the chosen NFR?

The prototype addresses the chosen NFR by providing a clean and structured interface for browsing the available meals.

The selection view lists every meal with its name, price and a short description, providing the user with a quick overview without overwhelming him with additional details like the ingredients.
The ingredients and recipe of a meal can be accessed by left-clicking on a meal in the list. This interaction is designed to be natural for most users, though some may not immediately recognize it.

Interactive elements, such as the update button or currency dropdown, are placed logically in a dedicated menu section and labeled clearly to reduce user confusion about their functionality.

Overall, the design focuses on simplicity and clarity, supporting an intuitive and efficient browsing experience for the user.
