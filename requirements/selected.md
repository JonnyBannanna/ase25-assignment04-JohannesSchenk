The prototype will focus on

- **FR:** As a customer, I want to browse meals offered by the catering service so that I can choose a suitable meal.
- **NFR:** As a developer, I want the platform to be easy to maintain and extend so that new meal types or dietary rules can be added without much effort.

Browsing available meals is one of the core functionalities of the service and therefore a reasonable scope for the first prototype. Since the prototype will surely not include all meals, focusing on the maintainability and extensibility mentioned in the NFR ensures that adding new meal types during the development process will be straightforward.
The FR and NFR are closely related because newly added meal types or dietary rules must be reflected in the browsing menu.
